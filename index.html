WHO WE ARE

Where Knowledge Meets Opportunity

APEX Jaipur • Rajasthan

Apex University is a multidisciplinary university located in Jaipur, Rajasthan, offering education across diverse academic fields.

Established under the Rajasthan State Legislature Act No. 27 of 2018, the university is recognized by the University Grants Commission under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act, 1956.

At Apex, education goes beyond textbooks. Students are encouraged to participate in practical learning, projects, research, internships, extracurricular activities and industry interactions.
